## Edit Policy

- `AGENTS.md` is user-owned and may only be modified by the user.
- `.agents/RULES.md` is shared and may be modified by both the user and the agent.
- If the two files conflict, `AGENTS.md` takes precedence unless the user explicitly says otherwise.
